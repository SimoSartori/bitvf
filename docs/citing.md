# Citing bitvf

If you use bitvf in published work, please cite the paper that describes the
method and its use:

- Sartori, S., et al. 2026, *The Back-in-time Void Finder: dynamical
  identification of cosmic voids through optimal transport reconstruction*,
  [arXiv:2601.15378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15378)

The reconstruction bitvf runs is otswap's; [its citing
page](https://simosartori.github.io/otswap/citing.html) lists what to cite for
it.
